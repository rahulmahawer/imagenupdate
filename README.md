Build a premium, cinematic, highly interactive website for an AI Product Imaging & Product Design Service.

Brand Name : Imagenmerce
capital word style logo.

All example products should use generic fictional product names only.

==================================================

PRIMARY BUSINESS IDEA

==================================================

This service helps businesses transform ordinary product reference photos into a complete, professionally presented product image system.

The customer may provide only one normal product/reference photograph.

We then transform that reference into a consistent set of high-quality product visuals such as:

01 — Hero Image

02 — Angled / Three-Quarter Image

03 — Detail / Material Close-Up

04 — Lifestyle Scene

05 — Alternate Lifestyle Scene

06 — Dimension / Technical Image

The website itself must VISUALLY demonstrate this transformation.

The visitor should understand within approximately 10 seconds:

ONE NORMAL PRODUCT PHOTO

↓

OUR AI PRODUCT IMAGING PROCESS

↓

6 PROFESSIONAL PRODUCT VISUALS

↓

A MORE CONSISTENT AND CUSTOMER-FRIENDLY PRODUCT PRESENTATION

Do not explain everything through paragraphs.

SHOW THE TRANSFORMATION.

IMAGE CREATION PRINCIPLES

==================================================

Whenever generated examples are shown, follow these principles:

1. REFERENCE IS SOURCE OF TRUTH

Never intentionally redesign the product.

2. SHAPE

Maintain the original silhouette and proportions.

3. MATERIAL

Do not arbitrarily turn one material into another.

4. COLOR

Keep product color visually consistent unless a color variation is intentionally requested.

5. CONSTRUCTION

Do not invent handles, legs, buttons, seams, openings or structural elements.

6. TEXTURE

Preserve visible texture and surface character.

7. BACKGROUND

Hero imagery uses a controlled white or near-white neutral background.

8. SHADOW

Use one subtle grounding/contact shadow rather than dramatic artificial shadows.

9. FRAMING

Keep consistent breathing room and product scale.

10. ALIGNMENT

Products within catalog-style imagery should visually share a consistent bottom baseline.

11. ANGLES

Use controlled camera directions rather than arbitrary perspectives.

12. DETAIL

Close-ups should reveal genuine visible characteristics, not hallucinated features.

13. LIFESTYLE

The environment may change dramatically.

The product should not.

14. DIMENSIONS

Never invent dimensions.

Only show dimensions supplied by the client.
